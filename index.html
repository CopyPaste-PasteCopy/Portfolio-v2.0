Storage Bay Refurbishment at Northeast Fence & Iron Works, Inc.

I led a month-long project to improve storage, organization, and workflow across our yard. The original plan involved refurbishing two storage bays for completed products from the powder coating department. However, as the benefits became clear, the scope expanded to six bays and the area directly across from them. Working with a small team, I led the on-site effort, primarily operating a forklift and Bobcat to remove disorganized materials and equipment, scrape away old paint, level the ground, apply fresh paint, and mark each bay with new stencils. The refurbished bays served as organized staging areas for the powder coating department and other teams. I also coordinated bay assignments for completed jobs and products prepared for installers each morning. The project brought structure to a previously congested space, improved visibility and workflow, and freed up valuable space in the powder coating yard. What began as a quick refurbishment developed into a meaningful operational improvement with benefits across multiple departments.

Powder Coating Departemnt Utility Closet Rebuild at Northeast Fence & Iron Works, Inc.

I led a small, dedicated crew in the complete renovation and reorganization of the powder coat department’s walk-in storage area. Over time, the space had become overcrowded and difficult to navigate, with disorganized inventory, excess touch-up spray, water damage, and a significant leak caused by a structural wall failure. Our team emptied and thoroughly cleaned the space, removed damaged materials, and poured a new concrete foundation to help prevent future water intrusion. We installed pallets to keep inventory off the floor and create a safer, more level walkway. We also introduced new storage containers and reorganized the inventory so materials could be easily identified and accessed. The completed renovation transformed an overdue maintenance issue into a clean, safe, and efficient storage area. I’m proud of the results and grateful for the hard work and commitment of everyone involved.

Sandblast and Powder Coating Storage Area Clean and Reorganization at Northeast Fence & Iron Works, Inc.

When our covered sandblasting and powder-coating storage area became overcrowded, finished products were being left exposed to weather, dirt, and potential damage. This created unnecessary rework, delayed deliveries, and added costs. I took ownership of the problem by using a forklift to relocate heavy equipment and materials, remove accumulated debris, and reorganize the available space. The improved layout created dedicated, protected storage for completed products while making the area cleaner, safer, and easier to navigate. Sandblasting removes rust, old coatings, and other contaminants from metal surfaces, preparing them for powder coating—a durable finish applied electrostatically and cured with heat. Once that process is complete, proper storage is essential to preserving the quality of the finished product. The result was more than a cleaner workspace. It was a practical improvement that helped protect product quality, reduce avoidable rework, improve workflow, and ensure finished items remained clean and ready for delivery.
